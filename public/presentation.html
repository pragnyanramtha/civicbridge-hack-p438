CIVICBRIDGE · HACK-P43802 / 07
Our solution

One place to find, ask,
report, and follow.

FIND + ASK

Guidance you can trace.

Search 15 clearly labelled sample guides. The assistant returns source cards and review dates, or says when an answer is unsupported.

REPORT

A clear next step.

Capture a subject, description, category, area, and optional small attachment. Get a unique reference and private tracking code.

FOLLOW

Progress made visible.

Citizens see saved timelines. Protected staff can categorise cases and move them through validated status changes.

All service content and example cases are sample data. There is no real government submission or official decision-making.

CIVICBRIDGE · HACK-P43805 / 07
Quality and uncertainty

“Done” needs evidence.

RETRIEVAL

Supported or unavailable.

Focused tests check primary sources in English, Telugu, and Hindi, follow-up context, and unsupported questions. No source means no invented citation.

WORKFLOW

History that stays consistent.

Transitions are validated. Request and audit updates share a transaction. Stale staff edits are rejected instead of overwriting newer progress.

HONEST MODE LABELS

No hidden simulation.

The UI distinguishes AI-generated, source-lookup, and unsupported answers. Guides, starter records, and non-integrated submissions are marked as demo content.

Build and unit checks are reproducible. The included integration script verifies persistence, ownership, attachments, staff changes, and citizen readback against a running environment.
